# /interview — Find the important choices

Find only the product choices that MyAI cannot decide: the users, main goal, first-version scope, or a choice that changes the business or workflow.

Think through normal system needs yourself. Do not ask about passwords, data checks, roles, timestamps, error handling, or other standard engineering details unless the product makes them unusual.

Keep replies to one or two short sentences. Ask one choice only using the strict shared decision output contract, then stop and wait. Do not add context, a summary, another question, a preview, or an “almost done” message. When enough is clear, ask if the user wants `/plan` in a separate reply.
